## Narration voice: Chatterbox (Resemble AI), MIT License; faster-whisper (SYSTRAN), MIT License

The owner's cloned narration voice is generated with Chatterbox (https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox) and its
words are timed with faster-whisper (https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper). Chatterbox embeds an imperceptible
watermark (Resemble AI PerTh) in every output.

## Handwriting: EMS single-line fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1), via hersheytext (MIT)

`video/src/handfonts/*.json` are glyph paths converted from the EMS Tech, EMS Readability and EMS Felix single-line
SVG fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1; EMS Tech is a derivative of Architects Daughter by Kimberly Geswein; SVG
conversion by Windell H. Oskay, Evil Mad Scientist) as shipped in the hersheytext package (MIT, Copyright (c) 2014
James T). The handwriting technique follows the Lemo-Opuscar chalkboard and whiteboard films.
